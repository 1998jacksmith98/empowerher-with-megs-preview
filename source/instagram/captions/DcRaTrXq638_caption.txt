Your body is not a trend. 🩷

We shouldn’t have to constantly adapt our bodies to whatever society has decided is “in” this year.

Skinny. Curvy. Tiny waist. Big bum. Strong. Lean.

The goalpost just keeps moving - and women are left feeling like they’re never quite enough.

And before anyone misunderstands the point - this isn’t about hating on slim bodies. It’s about questioning why one body type has to be THE ideal.

You don’t need to shrink yourself because the beauty standard changed.

You don’t need to earn your food.

You don’t need to punish yourself with exercise.

And you don’t need to chase a body that isn’t yours.

Let’s stop trying to fit our bodies into trends and start building a life where we actually feel good in them. 🫶🏼
