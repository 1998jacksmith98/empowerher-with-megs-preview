# Logo

**Yes — there is an official logo**, taken from the live site nav at https://empowerherwithmegs.com/.

## Files in this folder

- `logo-navigation.png` — **311×114, RGB PNG, 24,814 bytes.** Official header wordmark as used in the site `<img class="site-logo">` (`assets/images/logo-navigation.png`). Black rectangle, hot-pink italic “EMPOWERHER”, white script “With Megs x” between two thin white rules. This is the best logo file found. It is a **small raster**, not a vector.
- `favicon-32.png` — **32×32, RGBA PNG, 1,171 bytes.** The live favicon (`assets/images/favicon.png`). Too small to use as a logo. Looks like a tiny crop of the same wordmark. Do not upscale.
- `app-mockup-lenus-template.png` — **993×1200, RGBA PNG.** Official homepage “coaching app” mockup (`assets/images/App-V2-img.png`, alt: “A mockup of the coaching app showcasing the features.”). **This is a Lenus.io template**, not a custom photograph of her product. The middle phone greets “Hey Jenny!” (placeholder name) and lists tennis / stretching / smoothie tasks. The coaching-app screenshot in the middle phone does use her official About-V1 portrait. Use only as a “here is the kind of in-app experience the current site advertises” graphic, and **do not treat “Jenny” as a client**.

## What was not found

- No SVG, PDF, AI, or other vector logo.
- No white-on-transparent or pink-on-transparent version (the official file is on a solid black background).
- Larger “Logo.png / logo.svg / logo-white.png” paths on the domain returned Simply.com WAF/error HTML (7,305-byte fakes), not images — those were discarded.
- Instagram highlight covers could not be read anonymously, so no logo was taken from highlights.
- No Linktree / Beacons / Stan page for this handle (Linktree usernames empowerwithmegs, empowerherwithmegs and empower_with_megs are all “Page Not Found”).

**Ask Megs for a high-resolution or vector logo** before building a site that needs a sharp header, especially on retina screens. 311×114 will look soft if scaled up.
