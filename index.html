About me

I’m Megs.

I help women break free from all-or-nothing cycles. EmpowerHER is a full approach — mind, body and lifestyle. No toxic cycles, just real support and balance.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

Online coaching with bespoke workouts, tailored meal plans, in-app tracking and WhatsApp support. After you apply we do a 1-hour EmpowerHER Vision Call.

The goal is real results with minimal sacrifice — a lifestyle your future self will love.
